How Columbus Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
